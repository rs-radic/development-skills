# Exact ownership and final cleanup gate

Create/reserve this gate before any allocation. Follow the [workflow](../README.md); keep its executor and compact evidence outside the disposable targets.

- Same tracker task and linked native producer/consumer cards: `<references>`.
- Cleanup owner/executor and governing context/authority: `<verified-bindings>`.
- Exact resources registered **before allocation**, owner, producer and last consumer for each: `<inventory>`.
  - Worktree paths **and** Git registrations.
  - Task-owned local/remote feature or candidate refs, where allocated.
  - Preview application/routing mappings, isolated files/state and task-owned runtime stages.
  - Task-owned build/cache/scratch staging, including cleanup-owned staging.
- Protected resources: shared pools/identities/databases, other previews, active/unrelated work, committed baseline, unique unpublished source and compact evidence: `<explicit-protection-list>`.
- Completion reason: `<verified-production-success OR explicit-archive-cancellation>`.

## Safe retirement and evidence

1. Stop new allocation for explicit archive and coordinate pending/running producers. Ensure publication descendants cannot become eligible when a prerequisite is archived. Archive is the only cancellation signal and never undoes deployed code.
2. Wait for every owned producer and consumer to release disposable resources. Missing producer receipts do not prove no resources exist. Preserve unique unpublished source compactly before authorized retirement; do not discard it to obtain a clean status.
3. Remove only exact task-owned disposable resources. Never stop, recycle or delete a borrowed shared service. Do not broad-purge, guess process ownership or force disposal around a real holder.
4. Verify actual absence of every registered path, worktree registration, disposable ref, preview mapping and runtime/cache/build stage. Evidence: `<resource-by-resource-observation>`.
5. Retain compact source/revision, approvals, checks, CI/production or cancellation history outside those roots: `<evidence-reference>`.

An unexpected closed status, moved list, missing task or access failure triggers a **non-destructive hold**: suspend effects, invalidate usable release authority and preserve source/refs/previews. It is not authorization for these cleanup steps. Reconcile current task content, votes and revision before resuming; explicit archive may still cancel from the hold.

A live holder, unfinished producer, unknown ownership or still-required asset leaves this same gate unfinished. Retaining a whole preview/build/cache tree is not final cleanup. Record exact blockers and protected assets without calling them removed.

Normal completion remains in `technical approval` until actual production verification and this exact physical cleanup both pass; only then use `completed`. Cancellation completion reports safe retirement separately and does not imply a production rollback.
